ChaoticParty

Let the chaos begin!

ChaoticParty is a multiplayer party challenge game that gets everyone moving, thinking and doing ridiculous things. Race your friends to find objects, touch things, say funny phrases and complete silly real-world challenges — all guided by your phone.

Compete for points, embrace the chaos and see who comes out on top!
